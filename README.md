### 🎯 The Problem

In Bauchi:
- 40%+ voters struggle with English text-based INEC information
- Many can speak Hausa fluently but cannot read/write
- Fake news about PVC collection and 2027 election dates spreads faster than official info
- Result: voter apathy, exclusion — especially women, elderly, rural communities

### 💡 The Solution: MURYA

**Murya = Voice in Hausa.** A voice-first election helpdesk where citizens:

1. **Ask in Hausa by text or voice** — like WhatsApp, just press mic
2. **Get verified answers from INEC** — no AI hallucination, human verifies directly
3. **Listen 🔊** — Text-to-Speech for those who cannot read
4. **Share verified info** — one-click WhatsApp share to fight fake news

**Innovation:** Unified text + voice timeline, full Hausa dictionary detection, risk flags, offline-first design.
